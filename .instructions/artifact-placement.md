# Umbrella Artifact Placement

| Material | Location |
|---|---|
| Member repositories | `<NN>_u-<category>/<member-repository>/` gitlinks |
| Category purpose and exclusions | `<NN>_u-<category>/README.md` |
| Membership paths, remotes, branch hints | `.gitmodules` |
| Approved member revision | committed gitlink |
| Umbrella operating policy | `AGENTS.md` and `.instructions/` |
| Umbrella identity and attribution | root `README.md`, `LICENSE.md`, `NOTICE.md` |

Member implementation, operational records, credentials, and temporary output
stay outside the umbrella index. The umbrella must not duplicate member source,
store scan evidence, or reinterpret a member Gate verdict.
